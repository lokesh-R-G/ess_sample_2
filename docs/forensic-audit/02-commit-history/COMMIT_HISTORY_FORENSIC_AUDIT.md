# Commit History Forensic Audit (Consolidated)

## Executive Summary of Repository Evolution
Across 200 commits, ess_sample_2 evolved from an initial prototype into a multifaceted enterprise HRMS and ESS application.
Key evolutionary milestones include:
1. **Foundation & Dual Architecture Origin:** Early introduction of /backend followed by a parallel /app tree, causing architectural divergence.
2. **eSSL Biometric Integration:** Introduction of SOAP/TCP sync routines for biometric machines.
3. **Leave and Attendance Ledgers:** Transition from simple attendance tracking to complex policy engines with contextual shift resolution and permission ledgers.
4. **Payroll & Statutory Engine Revisions:** Evolution from static salary templates to dynamic CTC revision engines with PF, ESI, PT, and TDS calculation matrices.
